## 1.3 Reflection
Write 5–8 sentences:
- What was the most difficult part of crimping the cable?  
- Did the wiring work on the first try? Why or why not?

The hardest part was inserting the eight thin wires into the RJ45 connector while keeping them in the correct T568B order. The wires were easy to move out of place as I pushed them into the connector. I wired one end correctly, but I accidentally reversed the wire order at the other end. The cable tester showed an error on the first attempt. I replaced the incorrectly wired connector, arranged the wires in the correct order, and crimped it again. The cable passed the second test, and we used it to connect the two Raspberry Pis. Next time, I’ll check the wire order at both ends just before crimping.
